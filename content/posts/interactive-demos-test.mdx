Prose reads exactly as it always does. The moment a demo appears, it drops into
a consistent frame — a mono title bar, a rust dot, an "interactive" tag — so it
always reads as part of the site rather than an embed. Here is a demo that ties
back to the AsyncWrapper post: the decoy count is a dial you control.

<DecoyDial />

Back to prose immediately afterwards, at the normal reading measure. Demos don't
interrupt the flow; they punctuate it. The next one carries a product idea in a
single interaction — a confidential balance that only its owner can read.

<RevealToggle />

Some demos want more room than a column of text. The same frame takes a `bleed`
prop and breaks out past the reading measure on wider screens, while staying
contained on a phone.

And then the article simply continues. Every demo above is one line in the MDX
(`<DecoyDial />`), lives in `components/demos/`, and shares the same shell — so
adding the next one is a component, a register line, and a tag in the prose.
