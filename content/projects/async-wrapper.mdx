The layer underneath [Raycash](/work/raycash), written up for
[Zama's](https://www.zama.org) blog.

Wrapping an ERC-20 into an ERC-7984 confidential token is easy to get subtly
wrong. Do it in one transaction and you have published the link between the
public deposit and the encrypted balance it turned into. The amounts are
hidden and the graph is not, which is the sort of privacy that isn't.

AsyncWrapper splits the operation into `initWrap` and `finalizeWrap`, then uses
decoys and oblivious nullification so that finalising a wrap doesn't say which
deposit it came from. Screening still happens, which is what makes this usable
by a regulated on-ramp, but it stays non custodial. No operator holds the
funds, and none of them learns the amounts.

The interesting engineering is at the seams: the async boundary between the
public and confidential worlds, and testing state you are not allowed to read.
Written BDD-first with viem and Hardhat.
