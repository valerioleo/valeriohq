Won the IPFS/Filecoin
[Storage Mage](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/chaindouble-x6c9q) prize at
[ETHGlobal HackFS 2022](https://ethglobal.com/events/hackfs2022).

Dynamic NFTs want on-chain interactivity, but L1 gas makes that painful and L2
markets are thin. Chain Double treats the L2 token as the stunt double: it does
the heavy lifting (customisation, breeding, state), then a cross-chain message
pushes a deterministic IPFS CID up to the L1 twin so they stay visually
identical, without trusting a centralised metadata API.

The trick is that IPFS CIDs are computed from the data itself. A contract that
can build its metadata on-chain can also derive the CID, then ship that CID
across chains. Anyone can re-upload the bytes; the CID guarantees they match.
