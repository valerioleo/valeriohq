Writing a confidential contract from scratch means re-deriving the same
patterns every time: how to hold an encrypted balance, how to check a transfer
without learning the amount, how to grant and revoke the right to decrypt.
`confidential-primitives` collects those patterns into small, audited pieces you
can compose, so the interesting part of a contract is the only part you write.

It grew out of the work on [Raycash](/work/raycash) and
[AsyncWrapper](/writing/asyncwrapper-anonymous-on-ramp): the same primitives
kept reappearing, so they moved into one place. Open source, and meant to be
read.
