`deployoor` came out of a recurring annoyance. You deploy a contract, then
re-wire its address and ABI by hand into every app, script, and test that
touches it. This gives you one typed contract object to share everywhere, and
it works across Hardhat, Foundry, and viem.

The companion package, `@deployoor/testing`, is the part I use most. It runs
your deploys against a real in-memory EVM (TEVM) exposed as viem clients, so
there's no local node and no RPC, and your fixtures can't drift away from the
contracts they describe.

MIT licensed. `deployoor` is at v0.5.0 and `@deployoor/testing` at v0.3.0.
It's the most directly verifiable thing here, so read the code.
