An experiment in how little lending code you need if you lean on someone else's
settlement layer.

Pinkwhale takes two valid Seaport orders (borrower and lender) that name the
Pinkwhale contract as the recipient of the collateral NFT. Matching them on
Seaport escrows the token, then Pinkwhale posts two follow-up orders to cover
both endings of the loan: a repayment order (borrower buys the NFT back at
principal + interest before expiry) and a default order (lender claims it for
free after).

Collection-wide and trait-filtered loans came for free from Seaport's
consideration model. Never shipped as a product — the point was to see whether
a marketplace protocol could be bent into a lending one.
