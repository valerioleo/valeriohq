**Your money. Private. Yours.**

Raycash rebuilds the parts of a bank people actually use, a balance, a card, an
IBAN, money going in and out, except onchain and fully self custodial. The
difference that changes everything else is that the amounts are encrypted.
Balances and transfers stay confidential on the chain itself, using Zama's
fully homomorphic encryption, so the network can check and settle a payment
without ever seeing how much moved.

This is a product, not a demo. Confidential token contracts underneath, mobile
and web apps on top, and the wrap and unwrap flows in between. The
[developer docs](https://docs.raycash.xyz) are public. The app is in private
access.
