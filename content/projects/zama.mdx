Zama works on fully homomorphic encryption, which lets a computer calculate on
data it cannot read. That has been a real thing in papers for years. Zama's
work is making it fast enough to put in production.

I worked directly with the CEO and the leaders in protocol and cryptography
to shape future products. The work connected what the technology could do
with what people might find useful, and what could become a business.

One question kept coming up: what does FHE make possible at the scale of
ordinary people? I explored confidential money: self custody, encrypted
stablecoin balances, and compliance through selective disclosure and view
keys. The product challenge was to turn that into something familiar enough
to use without understanding the cryptography underneath.

That work became [Raycash](/work/raycash), which I now build full time. Zama's
FHEVM is still the cryptography underneath it.
