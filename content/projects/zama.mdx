Zama works on fully homomorphic encryption, which lets a computer calculate on
data it cannot read. That has been a real thing in papers for years. Zama's
work is making it fast enough to put in production.

I joined as Entrepreneur in Residence with one question to answer: what is FHE
actually good for, at the scale of ordinary people rather than demos? My answer
was banking. Not a privacy feature bolted onto a wallet, but the whole stack
rebuilt from first principles: self custody, confidential stablecoins, and
compliance that works by selective disclosure and view keys instead of by
publishing everyone's balance to everyone.

That work became [Raycash](/work/raycash), which I now build full time. Zama's
FHEVM is still the cryptography underneath it.
